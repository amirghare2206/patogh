4. Circle-aware reminder permissions
5. Opportunity Engine
6. Sports & Seasonal feed abstraction
7. Event Generation Templates & Rules
8. Memorial Center
9. Memorial verification
10. Memorial memories and guestbook
11. Memorial anniversary
12. Memorial-to-Golrizon linkage
13. Admin Time Engine panel
14. Backend RLS برای مموریال و مناسبت‌های شخصی
